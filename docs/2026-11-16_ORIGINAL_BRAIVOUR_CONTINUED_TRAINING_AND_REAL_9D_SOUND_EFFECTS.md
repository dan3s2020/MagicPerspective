## 5. Next unsolved mechanism, not another identical run

The existing pitch/loud world has reached exact optimal routes, so more episodes do nothing there. The real gap is temporal **conditional and spontaneous seven-dimensional sound movement**. Original acoustics contain many unchanged adjacent frames, so organism needs to *discover from its own consequences when a transformation should occur* instead of always applying remembered changes. A future proper test should incorporate actual previous seven-dimensional motion, full context, or higher-fidelity sound representation, and must outperform the honest inertia baseline on new cats BEFORE claims of accurate future meow reconstruction.

Current world still actuates motor coordinates 0,1 only; channels 2–8 are now causally drawn from original trained experience, NOT independently controllable nine axes. To reach full 9D embodied control, next experiment needs actual learned motor degrees of freedom or multi-slice native action experience, with strict autonomous audio trajectory and independent heldout/uncut validation.
