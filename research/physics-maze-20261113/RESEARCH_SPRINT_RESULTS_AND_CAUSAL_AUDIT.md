## Exact native-organism causal role (critical)
1. **Original V17 native actions genuinely executed:** `playExercise()` and its real native `Discoverer.learnStep()` recorded actions, world effects, conductance and attraction in the original maze environment.
2. **Original V17 `shapeGraph()` remained unchanged:** It navigates the research worlds' graph topologies and, in the Moyal and gravitational experiments, traversed candidate-hypothesis graphs.
3. **Do NOT credit unmodified original `Discoverer` with recovering the equations:** The high-accuracy numerical state predictions and model selection depend strongly on **added** mechanisms (sensor normalization, geometric action bodies, physical-rate interpolation, predetermined differential/monomial candidate grammars, coefficient fitting). In straight time-chain worlds the native shape graph merely follows a trivial path; an ablation replacing that path with a fixed action sequence would likely leave numerical accuracy unchanged. The quantification of this path-selection dependency has **not been executed** and cannot be implied by high R².
4. **Neither physical laws nor target answers entered the learner at test time**, but the external test worlds were built using established classical, quantum and relativistic equations. The finite operator grammar includes the known Moyal terms; its discovery is therefore **conditioned on researcher-supplied hypotheses**, not an unconstrained new-law search.
5. No Braivour GGUF intelligence assimilation was performed in these physics tests. The original V17 MagicPerspective organism, not an unrelated new LLM, was used.
