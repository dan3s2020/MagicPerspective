# Beyond globally linear laws: the original organism lives nonlinear physical action consequences
**2026-11-13 — additional genuine native V17 experiments, not a physics-law discovery.**

**Research problem:** The two-experience linear geometry body previously transferred oscillator, quantum interferometer, free Newtonian motion and Lorentz boosts but failed on a quartic anharmonic oscillator (MAE 2.028 on a different heldout set). Does the same original `Discoverer` control topology benefit when its **physical action body**, not the world law, gains causal state dependence from observed interventions?

## Implementation
- Real original `Discoverer` physically lived 160 original `playExercise` maze episodes. Its `shapeGraph` was kept unchanged and plans all future action chains.
- An internal `LocalFieldDiscoverer` physical memory retains individual **before/after interventions**; actual quartic equations are only in the **external world simulator**. It creates a local causal triangle from earlier observed phase-space states near its current state, using its lived action effects. 64, 256 or 1024 physically observed states; no algebraic formula for quartic force enters the agent.
- Four candidate body behaviors evaluated with separate **validation** initial states: nearest observed displacement, locally weighted displacement, local triangle consequence, hybrid. No test target is used for selection; the body method is frozen on 128 independent test worlds × 12-step futures.
- An isolated negative control permutes real physical action consequences among the same input states (destroys causal pairing). Other negative controls shift the unknown nonlinear force parameter and action duration.

## Observed heldout outcomes

| Experienced physical actions | Validated selected method | Mean vector error | Static mean error | Energy drift |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 64 | triangle | 0.413168 | 2.436147 | 0.394782 |
| 256 | triangle | 0.200302 | 2.436147 | 0.088425 |
| 1024 | triangle | 0.164898 | 2.436147 | 0.067490 |

`shapeGraph` called on every forecast; actual native actions are not a menu option. Same 160-episode trained original attraction/conductance learning is preserved in this experimental copy.

**Controls:**
- Shuffled causal experience (1024 intervention records): MAE **2.118828**.
- Different quartic-force strength, original training frozen: MAE **0.841791**.
- Different action duration, original training frozen: MAE **1.329596**.

This is **local geometric interpolation**, an existing technique implemented inside the original organism's physical action body, NOT a new mathematical law. The result is a causal mechanism for learning state-dependent dynamics from interventions; whether it forecasts long-horizon nonharmonic worlds robustly is limited by its training support, energy drift and physical-parameter shifts. It is not a quantum gravity solution. No actual laboratory physics measurements were obtained.
