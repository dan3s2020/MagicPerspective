## Outcomes

- **Independent organism initializations:** 8 seeds, each with 160 actual native `playExercise` training episodes. All achieved **12/12** most-likely quantum-detector classifications on the simple evenly spaced phase-cycle world.
- **Physically observed state connectivity, with hidden raw phase ordering:** 10 arbitrary sensory-ID permutations. A puppet performs 16 forward and 16 backward knob interventions; the resulting 32 directed transitions are recorded. The original `shapeGraph` then classified **120/120** heldout detector majorities without numerical phase coordinates or detector outcomes at the heldout points. Exact phase circle *connectivity was observed*, not given by sorting coordinates.
- **Critical failure control — unequal phase increments:** 40 unseen worlds where 16 knob steps have unequal angular widths, normalized to physical 0 and π at the two calibration positions. Same categorical connectivity, different physical predictions. Average **9.775/12**; range 6–12; only 7/40 fully correct.

## Scientific conclusion

A *topological circle* is not a *quantum phase metric*. Original `Discoverer.shapeGraph` can exploit observed adjacency to interpolate endpoint identity when the world parameter changes regularly, even with arbitrary symbols. The physically relevant phase increment per transition is not recoverable from adjacency alone when the increments are unequal. A quantum-law program needs additional **measured phase-sensitive or probabilistic causal observations** and phase/amplitude-aware internal state. This is a known-law benchmark and a project-level structural insight, **not** a new unification of physics.

No quantum law was trained from scratch, no independent experiment in a laboratory was conducted, and there is no proof of new physical dynamics.
