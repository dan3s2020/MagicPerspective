## Why we did not repeat the old STOP experiment

The previously completed original-Brain `stop-real-free` result saved **9.92%** real 4nt observations and improved within-dev400 P/N accuracy **63.25% versus 60.75%** on the exact same Brain forcibly using all three. This **failed the frozen 15% minimum savings**. A positive-cost native gate always GO; a naive gate feedback from one actually selected option cannot teach whether the *other* physical action would have been better. This newly tested experiment made the original Brain physically experience both stop and continue paths at training ONLY, then learn a relative value of observation.

## What was actually done

- Exact original native MagicPerspective V42 `Brain.live/Brain.predict`; no new trained classifier, no Python V5 organism, no GGUF, no original source changed.
- Exactly same authentic HS3D human AG acceptor 140nt P/N train data; original Brain initially learns 1,200 real examples × seven 4nt views, then 1,000 physical two-action delayed-reward calibration episodes.
- Another 500 real original train-calibration examples (250P/250N) are physically compared: STOP now (one view), STOP after one original Brain primitive move (two views), and original native GO twice (three views). All three P/N classifications come from the same Brain; original class truth consulted **only after all three physical outcomes**. This is an explicit counterfactual **supervision method** during training, not fully autonomous discovery.
- On the identical original Brain, two candidate gate actions per stage are rewarded from either comparative utility `correct − 0.08×extraViews`, absolute biological correctness, or independent destroyed feedback. Exactly 500 rows × two stages × two candidate actions × 8 bytes = **16,000 native lived symbols** in every arm.
- On already-seen genuine 400-record Train-derived dev, original Brain chooses its own STOP/GO at stage0 and stage1 with no access to unobserved destination DNA, true class or feedback; score true P/N after output.

## Causal diagnosis and practical implication

On the 500 compared real calibration cases, the actual relative-utility label **preferred STOP** in ~**75.6%** of stage0 comparisons (378/500) and **70.8%** of stage1 comparisons (354/500), at the specified observation price. This leads to a common global preference rather than *which early 4nt patterns require another look*. With only `[current4DNA, stage/own-P-bucket, stop-or-go]` in original C6, the organism lacks sufficient representation of the **different kinds of unresolved evidence**; it cannot generalize a conditional value-of-information map from that training.

This is a **failed attempt to transfer the Braivour V4 "knows it is complete" insight into native genome action policies**, not a failure of the prior V5 two-step delayed primitive results. Future research must test a *qualitatively different internal representation of unresolved questions* (or integrate real evidence across views) rather than retune the same binary stop cost, seed or calibration dose. Such a next test has NOT been executed here.

No independent gene-family source, AlphaGenome 2 accuracy, variant function prediction or Qwen/GGUF intelligence assimilation was measured.
