## Why this is the next scientifically necessary test

Two separate native abilities are now measured on real human DNA:
1. Original V42 C6 native `Brain.live/predict` learns two physical `again/undo/open/right/new/left` actions from delayed real outcome feedback. On independent-source human DRANet it reached 62.81% accuracy on its historical 1600 records vs same-Brain fixed64 57.81%; action selection was genuine, but its class readout used only final 4nt.
2. Original V42 C8 native `Brain.live/predict` FUSES two actual physical 4nt observations of the same genuine biological record, using its internally selected exact:149 C perspective; frozen DIFFERENT-source human n1600 reached 70.31% accuracy / 70.10% macro-F1 vs C8 single-view67.69% and mismatched-source66.19%. Same-original-Brain causal intervention on 1600 external changed 330 predictions when first real view alone was replaced, and accuracy decreased 70.31% to66.56%. But the view pair positions70/64 were human-chosen; the organism did not navigate to select them.

Neither experiment **alone** proves the original organism can **choose a sequence of physical views and integrate the chosen observations for one final prediction**. This precise gap, not re-running STOP/VOI or replaying V5 Python, is next.
