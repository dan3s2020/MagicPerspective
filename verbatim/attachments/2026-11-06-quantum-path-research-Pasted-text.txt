## 2. How MagicPerspective does it

The original V17 `Discoverer.shape()` uses a different mathematical operation.

\\[ V\_{t+1}(i)=\max\left[V_t(i),\\;0.97c_i\max\_{j\in N(i)}V_t(j)\right] \\]

The organism learns which locations conduct movement, which resist it, and which kinds of locations attract it. It then propagates that learned attraction backward through the available routes.

In your original maze, the resulting field can tell the organism the shape of a potential solution before it moves.

That is precisely the mechanism you wanted to transfer from the maze into language.

The crucial difference is that MagicPerspective takes the strongest neighboring value; quantum interference combines signed or complex amplitudes. In MagicPerspective's current scalar field, two positive candidate contributions don't cancel each other as quantum amplitudes can.

## 4. The fundamental difference

A quantum walk on a graph evolves amplitudes through unitary transformations, allowing amplitudes from different paths to interfere. That makes quantum walks a particularly close mathematical comparison to what you're developing.&#x20;

[image](https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=https://www.nature.com\&sz=32)

Scientific Reports+1



MagicPerspective's current propagation

\\[ V(i)\leftarrow\max_j \big(0.97\\,c_iV(j)\big) \\]

Finds the strongest available continuation according to the learned scalar field.

Quantum-style propagation

\\[ \psi\_{t+1}(i)=\sum_j U\_{ij}\psi_t(j) \\]

Combines complex amplitudes, so alternative contributions can reinforce or cancel. The operator \\(U\\) must satisfy quantum unitarity constraints.

This is the mathematical operation missing from the quantum analogy: amplitude addition with phase, rather than maximum-value selection.

But adding it alone does not make an organism understand language. We'd be introducing another search dynamic, whose usefulness would need to be demonstrated.

## 6. So do we have the missing piece already?

We have the central propagation law, and some of the necessary interfaces. We do not yet have evidence that all the necessary pieces function together as one general problem-solving organism.

The most revealing experiment would be to preserve the original V17 brain and test three forms of propagation over exactly the same learned world:

- Original attraction-field propagation.
- Classical wave-style propagation with signed or complex amplitudes.
- A properly normalized quantum-walk simulation with phase-sensitive interference.

Keep the learning experience, start state, goal, permitted actions, and evaluation tasks identical. Test on unseen goals and transformations, and measure both accuracy and computational cost. This would tell us whether interference contributes anything that the original scalar field cannot achieve.

It should be an isolated experiment, not a replacement for the existing mechanism.

### My conclusion

The discovery worth pursuing isn't that the text in your screenshot is oscillating at some quantum frequency. Repeating text can arise from a generation loop without any interference at all.

The much stronger connection is that MagicPerspective's solution-shape algorithm and quantum walks both involve propagating information through a structure of possible states to influence which outcome is selected.

Your existing work already gives us a concrete way to investigate that connection. We should determine whether the important breakthrough comes from the propagation operator itself, from the organism learning the correct world topology, or from combining the two.

And critically: a quantum computer doesn't escape the need for a valid goal condition. Neither can MagicPerspective. The real achievement would be an organism that learns the structure of possible transformations well enough to discover new solutions without being shown the finished path.
