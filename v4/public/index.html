SCIENTIFIC BOUNDARIES / DO NOT CONFUSE OBSERVATION WITH CLAIM

V4 includes a trainable local transition model, an adaptive choice between two fixed representational families, imagined connected trajectories and online corrections. It has not demonstrated a universal perspective, genuine 4D perception, or P = NP. Its beam search has an explicitly reported model-based fallback. The action scaffold is known but passability and costs are hidden; this is not fully structure-blind exploration. Positive conclusions require independent repeated-seed evidence.
