Explicitly distinguish “no path in finite known operator closure” from “search truncated” and from general impossibility.

Historical replay revisits **genuinely archived training-only** original sentences and compares old/current observed windows, learning missing cross-world one-edit operators from the same Brain. Replay is counted separately, not counted as newly observed source text. Source/target from heldout truth is never injected.
