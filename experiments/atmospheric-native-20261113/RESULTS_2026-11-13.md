## 1. Next-hour weather test — target unseen

Source: `experiments/atmospheric-native-20261113/native-weather-blind.mjs`.

Real ERA5 archived hourly 100 m wind direction, speed and precipitation 2025-10-01 through 2025-10-16, 384 hours in each of Berlin, Vienna, Budapest, Bucharest; 1,536 readings total. First 288 hours train each real native Brain through `Brain.live`; final 96 hours (384 station-timestamps) are evaluated causally: the next observation is not revealed until *after* the current prediction. Frozen fixed-origin 24h probe also recorded. Native `Brain.predict` checked against shuffled chronology, per-station training majority class and last-hour persistence.

| Forecast of 8-bin wind direction | Hits/384 | Accuracy |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Native Brain.predict | 146 | 38.02% |
| Shuffled training chronology | 148 | 38.54% |
| Per-station most frequent training direction | 190 | 49.48% |
| Last observed wind direction (persistence) | 307 | 79.95% |
| Frozen 24-hour probe without seeing future data | 44/96 | 45.83% |

The native organism did **not** outperform simple persistence, majority-class, or shuffled training controls.

## 2. Native complete three-symbol future shape

Same data and code; `brain.align.imagineAhead(3)` makes original Brain anticipate [next direction, next speed bin, next rain bin] **before** seeing truth.

| Metric | Hits/384 | Accuracy |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Direction | 142 | 36.98% |
| Speed interval | 109 | 28.39% |
| Rain/no-rain | 326 | 84.90% |
| All 3 correct simultaneously | 48 | 12.50% |
| Shuffled-chronology complete three-symbol future | 53 | 13.80% |
| **Always say no rain** | **374** | **97.40%** |
| **Keep prior hour speed bin** | **335** | **87.24%** |

The superficially high 84.90% rainfall score is **worse than the trivial 97.40% always-dry control**. Likewise the native speed bin score is far worse than persistence. Do not report these as successful meteorological forecasting.

## 3. Real measured-wind-directed original shapeGraph maze

Source: `experiments/atmospheric-native-20261113/wind-field-shapegraph.mjs`.

16 real ERA5 geographic grid locations, 96 hourly records 2025-10-01..04. One discrete cell is connected to a neighboring cell when the observed **100 m local horizontal wind-to-vector** points sufficiently toward it and speed is at least 0.5 m/s. The graph is an intentionally simplified static 2D wind-alignment maze, not the physical flight path of an air parcel.

The same actual `Brain` first learns attraction/conduction through 4 original `brain.playMaze` episodes (no manually seeded trust coefficients); the first half of observed weather states is lived using `Brain.live`. The identical `Brain.disc.shapeGraph` plans on the time-indexed wind graph *before moving*. The *current* wind graph and goal are visible, NOT an unseen future, so this is a pathfinding test, NOT an unseen weather forecast. Independent BFS reachability evaluator is run only **after** native planning and never chooses the organism's moves.

- 96 evaluated goal-directed wind graph episodes (32 snapshots × 3 target cells).
- 14/96 graphs had a physically allowed discrete path from start to target.
- Original `Discoverer.shapeGraph` found a valid route in **14/14 reachable cases**, with **0 false positives**.
- All 14 successes occurred in held-out period (14/48 examples), with no path for the 34 unreachable held-out examples.
- Per-target reachable/solved: east 2/2, north 11/11, northeast 1/1.
- Native physical maze priors after 4 native episodes: target attraction 0.5, floor conduction 0.9911.

**Interpretation**: Positive evidence that the original graph planner can apply its goal-directed shape navigation *to real observed weather-derived graph topology*, rather than merely simulated maze geometry. But the grid and edge validity come from an externally specified rule and observed weather; no novel wind physics, source localization, future prediction or learned atmospheric dispersion mechanism is demonstrated. The held-out hours do not prove causal generalization from weather absorbed into brain (because edge graph is supplied and graph planner acts on it).

## Threat and safety limitations

This is NOT radioactive fallout forecasting, a nuclear consequence model, a dose model, a 3D Lagrangian weather transport model, an air-safety verdict, or any evidence that a nuclear attack has happened or will happen.

A real protective application would require: 3D time-varying winds at multiple pressure levels, validated atmospheric trajectory/dispersion/deposition models such as NOAA HYSPLIT, confidence intervals, verified radiological monitoring, and qualified public-health assessment. Do not use these results for decisions to evacuate or shelter.
